Hubness explorer

k-occurrences of points uniform on the square [−1,1]²

Parameters

The point

position
Ok:N(x)

Discrete experiment one random sample of N points

Each point in turn (top-left to bottom-right) picks its k nearest neighbours: they turn green and their counter goes up by 1. At the end, points are coloured by their final count, the k-occurrence.

Empirical check for the current x

Draws N uniform points per experiment and counts how many have x among their k nearest neighbours.

Display

Expected k-occurrence of every x, computed by midpoint integration over c; only 1/8 of the points are computed thanks to the square's symmetry.
